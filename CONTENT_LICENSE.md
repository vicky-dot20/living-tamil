## Third-party material

Ancient original works may be in the public domain, but modern editions, translations, commentaries, photographs, recordings, transcriptions, and database arrangements may have separate rights. Their original licence and attribution remain controlling. A source repository's software licence does not necessarily license every cultural text returned by its API.

API responses are not relicensed merely because the application displays or caches them. Consult [docs/SOURCES.md](docs/SOURCES.md) and the metadata attached to each discovery before reuse. When rights are unclear, link to the source and do not copy the material into the repository.
