## Hook
One of Tamil’s most quoted lines is often used as a simple declaration of universal belonging. The poem around it asks harder questions about fortune, suffering and human dignity.
## Original
யாதும் ஊரே யாவரும் கேளிர்.
## Simple
எந்த ஊரும் நமக்கு அந்நியமல்ல; எல்லா மனிதர்களும் உறவினரைப் போன்றவர்கள் என்ற பரந்த பார்வையை இந்த வரி திறக்கிறது.
## English
Every place is our town; everyone is our kin. The line opens a wider reflection on shared humanity and the changing conditions of life.
## Context
Quoting only the opening can flatten the poem. A contextual reader should always let users move from a famous fragment back to its larger argument.
## Today
The line appears in civic, political and global settings. Its ethical challenge is larger than celebrating cultural pride.
