# Community code of conduct

Living Tamil welcomes contributors across language ability, geography, caste, religion, gender, age, disability, and professional background. Discussion of language, identity, history, and heritage can be personal; disagreement must remain evidence-led and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat contributors, users, communities, authors, and traditions with dignity.
- Critique claims and sources rather than people or identities.
- Welcome corrections and clearly separate evidence, interpretation, and uncertainty.
- Use inclusive language and accept that Tamil usage varies across regions and communities.
- Respect privacy, attribution, copyright, and contributor boundaries.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, casteist or discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, deliberate misinformation, plagiarism, doxxing, or sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing private or harmful details. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should be handled confidentially and without retaliation.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
